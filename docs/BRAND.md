## Exact generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a NEW final production app icon for Thai Daily News, a premium contemporary editorial news-reading application. Reject ornate, metallic, ribbon, radio-waves/RSS, embossed and old newspaper logo aesthetics. Design a very minimal bold architectural white editorial symbol: two asymmetric vertical page-like slabs and a small warm muted champagne square accent, combining into a subtle abstract N / open-book / news-window silhouette. The mark must feel like a high-end independent digital publication and a meticulously designed productivity app in 2026. Pure flat shapes, mathematical optical balance, quietly confident Swiss editorial design, clear negative space, no gradients, no metallic materials, no lighting, no shadows, no texture. Near-black midnight blue solid full-bleed square background #0B1424. Main mark warm off-white #F1EEE6 with ONLY one tiny champagne #D6B775 accent. Centered compact symbol at 55% of canvas width, generous safe margin for Android adaptive icons. 1024x1024 square, no rounded corners baked in. One icon only, no letters, no words, no mockup, no frame, no watermark. It must be exceptionally simple and recognizable at 24 pixels. Make it a distinctive polished app identity, not a decorative crest.
